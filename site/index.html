Method & provenance
The engine starts from each recording’s saved header and advances without forcing later reference states. The first reported unsupported update or checkpoint mismatch stops that case.
A mismatch is detected at a stored checkpoint; the divergence may have begun earlier. The final uncheckpointed tail is not independently verified. Ignored command counts are shown as a further verification limit.
Ordinary engine revisions use a frozen core of 500 and 500 additional recordings. The combined 1,000 always contains the core. IDs and recording hashes stay fixed even as failures are fixed. These sets emphasize engine features and failures; their success rate does not estimate the whole corpus’s success rate.
The original 5,604-reference view remains available for historical comparisons. Parse and structure errors remain in the totals.
